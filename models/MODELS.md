`ood_stats.npz` are the files used for every result in `docs/EVALUATION.md`.

## Not included

- **TensorRT `.engine` files and timing caches.** An engine is tied to one GPU
  and one TensorRT version and must be rebuilt on the target:
  `scripts/export_yolo_trt.sh` (block 1), `scripts/build_block2_trt.sh`
  (block 2). Without an engine the pipeline runs the PyTorch path and says so
  in `warnings`.
- Training checkpoints of the twelve localizer recipes and of block 2.
